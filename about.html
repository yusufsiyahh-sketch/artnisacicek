Hello! Bonjour! Hallo! Hola! Ciao! Merhaba!

I am Nisa, a third-year Graphic Design student at Yalova University; I previously graduated from the Visual Arts department of Yalova Fine Arts High School.

I pursue my artistic practice across diverse fields—such as portraiture, sculpture, spatial art, and digital art—without limiting myself to a single discipline. In addition to my portrait work, I create paintings and murals for interior spaces, drawing inspiration from the unique cultural and regional art forms of various geographies and reinterpreting these elements through my own artistic lens.

You can contact me here to learn more about my digital or physical artworks, ask any questions you might have, or simply say hello.
